rgb geometry live 1.8.0 // Android 13 or newer

  1. Open the Android APK or refresh rgb geometry live in klobstore. Updating keeps your Home and Lock settings.
  2. Open rgb geometry live, select Home or Lock, and choose your model, pattern, framing, colors, and outlines.
  3. For a live wallpaper, tap Preview Home live or Preview Lock live, then choose a destination in Android’s picker. Each screen uses its own layout. For a still, tap Set Home still or Set Lock still.

Live and still in the Android app. No web PNG or SVG download.
