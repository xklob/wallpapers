# RGB geometric wallpapers: solid clipped-edge edition

720 lossless PNG wallpapers in total:

- 480 standard files: four source designs × 60 device/orientation presets × two styles.
- 240 camera-outline files: four designs × 30 camera profiles × two styles.
- Pixel Tablet and seven iPad size families, in portrait and landscape.
- Galaxy Z Fold7, Z Flip7, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, OnePlus Open, OPPO Find N5,
  HONOR Magic V5 and Motorola Razr Ultra 2025, with both display sizes and
  both orientations.

The original style and padded RGB-frame style are included throughout.

## Designs

1. 01_honeycomb_bright: first supplied image, brighter dense honeycomb.
2. 02_hexagons: second supplied image, spaced hexagon outlines.
3. 03_honeycomb_subtle: third supplied image, subtler dense honeycomb.
4. 04_cubes: fourth supplied image, spaced wireframe cubes.

Every design contains original/ and rgb_border/ folders under each device group.
The original style has a small black safety margin. The RGB style includes a complete rainbow
frame and a black gutter between the frame and the pattern. Frame corners and
stroke thickness stay consistent across aspect ratios. Black backgrounds and
the original shapes' proportions are retained.

## Rendering and setup

Partial hexagons and cubes at the pattern's rectangular crop boundary are
retained. Each cut-off contour is closed with one thin solid segment along the
crop boundary, matching the adjacent cell's color and line weight. Separate
cells remain separated; the caps do not span the black gaps between them.
At a cropped corner the closure follows the two intersecting crop edges.
There are no dotted or dashed lines.

The four designs, all original resolutions and both styles are retained. The RGB frame
remains solid, with black padding between it and the pattern. All source
geometry is fitted without distortion. Original source files are 1254×1254;
larger files use Lanczos upscaling and retain the source's inherent detail.

On desktop, choose Fill. On phones, disable extra wallpaper zoom, scrolling,
or automatic extension where possible and keep the image centered so all four
border edges remain visible. Rounded display corners, camera cutouts and system
overlays can cover small portions of a wallpaper.

## Frame prompt

Preserve the exact source RGB geometric line pattern on black. Add one complete
crisp rectangular rainbow border, inset near the edge, with square corners and
generous black padding between the border and pattern. No glow, decorations,
text, logo, or device mockup.

## Camera-outline variants

Camera-ring files are stored in camera_outline/ or camera_outline_estimated/
inside each base style folder. The ring surrounds the front-camera hole and
keeps a small black clearance around it. Both plain and RGB-frame styles have
camera-ring variants. Book-style foldables include inner and cover profiles;
flip-phone cover displays show rear cameras, so they retain their standard
wallpapers. iPhones use a pill-shaped cutout rather than a circular punch hole
and retain their standard wallpapers in this edition.

Pixel 9, 9 Pro and 9 Pro XL rings use Google's published native-pixel cutout
centers and radii. All other ring profiles are ESTIMATED, including Samsung
and foldable profiles. Their filenames explicitly include _estimated. Screen
resolution alone does not establish the hardware cutout position, so these
profiles may need adjustment. The included offline Camera_Ring_Adjuster.html
lets you change the center, radius and size, then export a matching wallpaper.

Landscape foldable ring variants assume the camera rotates to the left side
of the image. Keep wallpaper zoom/scrolling/automatic extension disabled when
using a camera-outline wallpaper. Display scaling and wallpaper placement can
affect alignment even with a published native-pixel profile.
